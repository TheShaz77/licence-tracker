Comparison of Banking Services Tool
    

 

This Comparison of Banking Services Tool is established to enhance the transparency of bank fees and charges; to promote competition and to enable the public to compare fees and charges on an apples-to-apples basis for services related to Housing Loans, Car Loans, Wallet Services, and Basic Bank Accounts (BBAs). The Bank of Namibia hopes that by launching this comparison tool, consumers would get a better understanding of financial services and products available to them in order to make informed decisions.



*DISCLAIMER*

The information displayed on this Comparison of Banking Services Tool (Tool) are indicative, based on your inputs.
The use of this Tool is at own risk. The Bank of Namibia (Bank) does not accept responsibility for any loss or damage of any nature that may be caused or brought about, directly or indirectly, by the use of this Tool or reliance on any information displayed herein.
While every effort is made to keep information up to date and accurate, any computation quoted on this Tool must be confirmed by your respective bank.
The Bank gives no warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, reliability and completeness of any information or computation provided through the use of this Tool.

Interest Rates Explained:
Repo rate

Repurchase rate: Rate at which the private (sector) banks borrow namibian dollars from the Bank of Namibia.

Prime overdraft rate (predominant rate)

Benchmark rate at which private banks lend out to the public.
