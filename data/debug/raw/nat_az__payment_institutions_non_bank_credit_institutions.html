Non-Bank Credit Institutions
A-A+

Non-bank credit institution (NBCI) – is a specialized credit institution engaged in granting loans under a special permit (license) and other activities specified in the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan on Non-bank Credit Institutions.


The Central Bank maintains a public register of NBCIs, branches and representative offices.
