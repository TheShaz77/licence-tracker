Banks
A-A+

 

The banking system of the Republic of Azerbaijan consists of the Central Bank of the Republic of Azerbaijan, as the financial markets supervisory authority, and credit institutions.


Bank – is a legal entity that attracts deposits and other returnable funds from individuals and legal entities, issues loans on its behalf and from its own funds, as well as makes transfers and cash settlements in aggregate at a clients’ requests.


The Central Bank maintains a public register of banks, branches, departments and representative offices.
