Register of E-money Institutions

The register of electronic money institutions is a database of:


  • e-money institutions authorised by the CBCG to issue e-money; 
  • branches through which e-money institutions issue electronic money and/or provide payment services; and
  • agents to which electronic money institutions have entrusted the provision of payment services.

Currently there is no electronic money institution registered.
