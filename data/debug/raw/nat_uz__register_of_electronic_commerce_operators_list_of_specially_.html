List of specially authorized crypto-exchanges for providing a special tax regime to foreign citizens

40703

In accordance with the Decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. DP-180 dated October 4, 2025, “On Measures for the Implementation of a Special Tax Regime for Foreign Citizens,” and the Order of the National Agency for Prospective Projects of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. 49 dated June 1, 2026, a list of specially authorized crypto exchanges has been approved.
Foreign citizens who have received a decision from the Commission on Granting the Special Tax Regime for Foreign Citizens are permitted to open crypto wallets on these exchanges.
